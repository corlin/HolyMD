This is a demo site for [HolyMD](https://github.com/corlin/HolyMD), a GEO-native, open-source engine for independent websites.

Sign in to the admin at `/admin/login` with the demo credentials from `compose.yaml` to explore the writing studio, publish preflight, and GEO dashboard.
